25+Years of Craft
Since 1998

Crafted with devotion

What began as a two-bench workshop in Mumbai is now a house trusted by over twelve thousand patrons. Every Avani piece still passes through the hands of our master goldsmiths — drawn, cast, set and polished under one roof.

We measure luxury in the details others never see: the weight of a clasp, the curve of a bangle, the way light finds a table facet.

Ethically Sourced

Certified, conflict-free diamonds and responsibly mined gold from verified partners.

Handcrafted In-House

Forty master karigars shape, set and finish every piece in our Mumbai atelier.

Craftsmanship

Where hand meets heart

A single Avani ring passes through eleven pairs of hands — from wax carver to setter to polisher. We still favour the lost-wax method and hand engraving, because machines cannot feel a curve.

Lost-Wax Casting

Each design begins as a hand-carved wax model, cast in small batches.

Hand Engraving

Our engravers finish temple pieces with motifs passed down generations.

Triple Quality Check

Purity, setting and finish are inspected three times before hallmarking.
