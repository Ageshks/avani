Product details and care

Hand-finished in our Mumbai atelier using the lost-wax method. The centre diamond is accompanied by its grading certificate, and the band carries the BIS 750 hallmark and Avani maker's mark. Supplied in a lacquered rosewood box with a certificate wallet.

Clean gently with warm water, mild soap and a soft brush; pat dry with a lint-free cloth. Remove before swimming, gardening or applying lotions. Bring your ring to any Avani atelier once a year for complimentary professional cleaning and prong inspection.
